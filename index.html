Grace for Dinner Guidelines

Grace for Dinner provides a free prepared meal and groceries once a month to anyone in need.

Our mission: Offer Food, Connection, and Hope

When & where

  • 2nd Saturday of each month, 4:00–5:30 PM
  • Grace Presbyterian Church, 57 Sand Hills Road, Kendall Park, NJ 08823

Reservations

  • Meal reservations are required, made by the Monday before or earlier (check the monthly flyer — the RSVP deadline is sometimes earlier)
  • Call 732-297-9182, email office@gracepcsb.org, or use the reservation link on the website
  • Please complete the registration form before or at your first visit — available on the website

Meal & grocery limits

  • One meal per person; maximum 6 meals per household
  • Approximately 250 meals per month, while supplies last
  • Each household present may select two standard-size bags of groceries
  • You may not pick up meals or groceries for someone else unless they've confirmed and registered — limited supplies

Day-of schedule

  • Numbers are given out around 3:30 PM
  • While waiting, sign up for next month's meal, wait in your car or on the porch, or join a fitness class (check-in at 3 PM, when available)
  • Doors open at 4:00 PM — no early entry or pickup, as we need time to prepare
  • If you arrive early, please sign in at the door; guests are invited in by number when doors open
  • Arriving after 4:00 PM? Sign in at the check-in table inside
  • Didn't pre-order? You can still get groceries and check for extra meals around 5:15 PM
  • Picking up for someone else too? List their name separately
  • Meals can be eaten on-site or taken home — we'd love for you to stay!

About the food

  • Meals are purchased from local restaurants and caterers at a discount — please support them if you can, and use caution if you have food allergies
  • Groceries are generously provided by Replenish Food Bank, Middlesex County

Cancellations & no-shows

  • Can't make a reserved meal? Call by the day before so we may be able to arrange an alternate pickup time — this is not guaranteed, so please call as early as you can
  • More than two no-shows per year will pause pre-order privileges
  • You may occasionally be asked to complete a brief survey — it helps with funding and improvements

Questions or suggestions?

  • Contact Lori Jarvis, Program Director, or Pastor Debbie Paton through the church office
  • 732-297-9182 · office@gracepcsb.org
Thank you for your patience, kindness, and respect for your neighbors 💙
